Behind closed doors
the book of resentments
this book is as follows untrue and unkept
and handed onto you the reader
all though untrue it is however based on real life experiences actual events true story's and witness accounts every lie taken from

become nothing but fiction they are written with the darkest twisted imagination humanly possible
with no moral compass whatsoever only betrayal heartbreak and bitter resentments without so much as single bit of loyalty or trust
